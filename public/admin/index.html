Subscribers

Copy puts confirmed subscribers only on your clipboard. Paste them into the BCC box so people cannot see each other's addresses, and send in batches if your email provider limits recipients. Each email must include an unsubscribe link: the CSV has a personal link for every person.

Import a list of emails

Paste addresses (or a whole CSV export, for example from Shopify) or choose a file. Anyone already on the list is skipped, and anyone who has unsubscribed is never added back.

Send an email

Write a message and it goes to every confirmed subscriber, one email each, with their own unsubscribe link and the company address added at the bottom. People who are waiting to confirm or have unsubscribed never get it.

First press Send me a test and check it in your inbox (and junk folder). The send button only works once you have tested the exact message you are about to send.
